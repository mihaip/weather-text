# Privacy Policy

## Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is handled in Weather Text.

## We Collect No Personal Information

The developer does not collect, use, save, or have access to any of your personal data.

Weather Text requires location access to request weather forecasts from Apple WeatherKit and to determine a display name for your location using Apple's geocoding service. This information is sent to Apple, but is not sent to or accessible by the developer.

Your saved work location, settings, and dismissed weather alerts are stored on your device and are not uploaded to the developer or any developer-operated service.

Weather Text does not include accounts, advertising, or analytics.
